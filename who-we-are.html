Who We Are

VL Consulting provides independent project management and strategic consulting services to organizations across technology, life sciences, and education. The business supports planning, coordination, and execution of R&D projects, clinical and operational programs, innovation initiatives, process improvement, and AI-enabled transformation. Services are delivered on a self-employed basis to Dutch and international clients, primarily through remote consulting, contract-based work, teaching, tutoring, and mentoring.

Profile

Project Management & Strategy Experience

VL Consulting is led by Vé Léandre, Ph.D., whose experience spans project management, portfolio strategy, scientific innovation, and cross-functional coordination. Vé worked at Genentech (Roche Group) embedded in teams spanning the full product life cycle—from research and development to product development and commercial strategy—where she developed experience navigating and leading complex, multi-million-dollar projects and initiatives. At Brown University and the Providence VA Medical Center, she gained experience managing international and academic–industry collaborations, as well as exposure to the legal and operational aspects of the patenting process through work with the technology ventures office.

This combined experience enables a deep understanding of the unique coordination, governance, and strategic challenges organizations face across both industry and academic environments. Explore Strategy & Projects case areas.

Perspective

AI, Innovation & Human-Centered Technology

VL Consulting approaches AI as a powerful tool for innovation, discovery, communication, and organizational transformation. When paired with human-centered design, originality, and responsible implementation, AI can help organizations improve how they analyze information, solve problems, and create value.

Vé’s experience includes planning an informatics conference, organizing thought leaders in the San Francisco innovation community, contributing to work at the intersection of accessibility and breakthrough technology, and supporting international AI firms through rubric design, model evaluation, and assessment of generative AI performance on complex STEM, imaging, and reasoning tasks.

Education

Teaching, Tutoring & Mentorship

Education has been central to Vé’s work since high school, continuing through undergraduate study, graduate school, and professional scientific training. VL Consulting offers teaching, tutoring, and mentoring support for advanced learners in scientific, technical, biomedical, and analytical disciplines.

Areas of teaching expertise include biomedical engineering, polymer science, tissue engineering and biomimicry, drug delivery and bioabsorption, research methods, scientific communication, and statistical analysis.
