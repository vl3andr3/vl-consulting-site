About

VL Consulting provides independent project management and strategic consulting services to organizations across the technology, life sciences, and education sectors. The firm specializes in the planning, coordination, and execution of complex research and development programs, as well as innovation-driven initiatives.

Services include cross-functional program management, organizational strategy, process optimization, and the implementation of data-driven and AI-enabled solutions. VL Consulting partners with both early-stage and established organizations to improve execution, align stakeholders, and drive measurable outcomes.

The firm engages in freelance and contract-based work with international clients, including companies operating in artificial intelligence, data annotation, and digital technologies.

In addition, VL Consulting offers educational services, including teaching and tutoring advanced university and graduate-level students in scientific, technical, and analytical disciplines.

All services are delivered primarily through remote engagements, serving Dutch, American, and international clients.
